## Mathematical predecessors

- **Chaim Goodman-Strauss**, *A Pair of Aperiodic Tiles in Eⁿ* (1999):
  [author preprint](https://strauss.hosted.uark.edu/papers/NDimPair.pdf).
  The coarse chair hierarchy and parent-recognition mechanism have this
  predecessor. The [markings comparison](strong/review/GOODMAN_STRAUSS_COMPARISON.md)
  explains the scope of our reconstruction. The paper is linked, not
  redistributed as a full PDF in this repository.
- **Schmitt, Conway and Danzer** developed the earlier SCD construction.
  Our implementation draws on Michael Baake and Dirk Frettlöh,
  [*SCD Patterns Have Singular Diffraction*](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1842355)
  (2005). SCD permits screw symmetry.
- The tutorial and literature reports cite further sources at the claims
  they support. References do not transfer the authors' rights to this project.
