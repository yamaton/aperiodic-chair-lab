Can identical blocks fill space while making every repeating arrangement
impossible? Follow a seven-cube chair from local matching rules to a hierarchy
at every scale, then explore what the mathematics means for physical models.

**[Read the illustrated tutorial →](tutorial/)**

The tutorial introduces the required ideas for readers familiar with
undergraduate vectors, matrices and elementary calculus. It includes
ten figures you can enlarge and nineteen exercises with worked answers.

## What has been checked?

The discrete integer-grid model has Lean proofs of unique grouping, legal
deflation, translation exclusion and a bound of 24 proper grid symmetries.
The curved solid's arbitrary-placement and existence arguments are written
mathematics outside that completed formal development. Neither meshes nor
printed approximations inherit the exact-solid claims.

The chair construction has substantial precedents, and the discrete matching
system agrees with Ioannis Tsiokos's Chair44 construction under the recorded
coordinate correspondence. The physical surfaces differ. See the tutorial's
[evidence and attribution section](tutorial/#what-has-been-established-and-where-to-read-more)
before interpreting the research claims.
