An activity, and an ongoing research project

The activity models patterns and arrows on a grid. A successful local attachment does not prove an infinite tiling, and the activity does not simulate physical insertion or the detailed curved surfaces.

What has been checked, and whose work is this based on?

The integer-grid model has Lean proofs of unique grouping, legal deflation, translation exclusion, and a bound of 24 proper grid symmetries. The arbitrary-placement and existence arguments for the curved solid remain written mathematics outside that completed formal development. Meshes and printed approximations do not inherit the exact-solid claims.

The discrete matching system agrees with Ioannis Tsiokos’s Chair44 construction under the recorded coordinate correspondence; the physical surfaces differ. This project was developed with substantial AI assistance. AI reviews are not independent human review.
