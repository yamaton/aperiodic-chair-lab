## Preserving evidence while updating conclusions

The frozen candidate and old PDF/HTML/ZIP files are not rewritten to insert
new notices. This guide supplies the surrounding context. If redistributed
separately, include the current provenance and licensing notices and explain
that the packet is historical. A future current review packet should receive
a new version and new hashes.

The public presentation should lead with the tutorial, current comparison,
formal scope and experiments. Preserving the notebook does not require
leading readers through every abandoned approach before those entry points.
